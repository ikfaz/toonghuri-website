ABOUT

A small studio that grew out of a cartoon channel.

ToonGhuri Studio helps YouTube creators publish their videos in more languages: AI dubbing, subtitles, translated titles, descriptions and chapters, thumbnail and title variants, publishing, and A/B tests where YouTube allows them.

Where it started

The studio began with our own animation channel, ToonGhuri. To bring stories like Pip the Little Cloud to more viewers, we built a workflow that dubs a Bangla original into 11 audio languages and publishes everything in YouTube Studio. You can hear the result by opening that video’s Settings → Audio track menu.

Today we bring the same workflow to creators and studios who want every upload to reach viewers in their own language, without adding hours to their week.

How the work is done

ToonGhuri Studio combines an AI production pipeline with a team that oversees every job. The pipeline handles transcription, translation, AI voice generation, subtitles, metadata, thumbnail variants, publishing and test setup; our team checks the work and steps in when needed.

  • The dubbed voices are AI-generated, not human voice actors.
  • You invite us through YouTube Studio’s channel permissions. You never share your password, and you can remove access at any time.
  • We confirm your package and final total before production starts.

Our promise

We promise the work, not the views. Every item in your package is delivered; how viewers respond is up to them, and we will show you what happened, including tests with no clear winner.
