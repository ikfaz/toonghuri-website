GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers.

Can every video use A/B testing?

We check your channel and video eligibility first. YouTube’s native tests require advanced features and do not support Shorts, private, age-restricted or Made for Kids videos. We set up eligible tests and review the outcome, including when there is no clear winner. See YouTube’s requirements ↗

Is this only for animation?

No. Pip is our featured example. Send content from any YouTube niche so we can review what it needs.

How is my quote calculated?

Dubbing depends on video length and languages. Packaging and testing depend on the titles, thumbnails, publishing work and follow-up included. You get one clear total before production starts.

Do I share my YouTube password?

No. For uploads, you invite our team through YouTube Studio’s channel permissions.
