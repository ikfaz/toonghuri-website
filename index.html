A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING.

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Is this only for animation channels?

No. The featured demo is animation, but you can send content from other YouTube niches for a sample. We’ll review your video and the voices it needs.

Can I start with just a few languages?

Yes. Custom offers start at 2 languages and can scale to 100. The standard monthly plans include 12 languages. Tell us which languages suit your audience.

What counts as an original video minute?

The length of the video you send us. A 10-minute video dubbed into 12 languages uses 10 original minutes and produces 120 dubbed minutes. Your plan’s volume is based on original minutes.

Do I need to share my YouTube password?

No. You invite our team through YouTube Studio’s channel permissions when you choose an upload service. You keep control of your channel.

What is included in the free sample?

A 90-second segment of your video dubbed into the agreed languages, so you can review the sound and character voices before choosing a paid plan.
