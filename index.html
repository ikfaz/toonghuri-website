Good to know

Quick answers.

Anything else? Email studio@toonghuri.com.

What does the free sample include?

A free 90-second dub of your own video in one language you choose, so you can hear the result before paying anything. One free sample per new customer; no plan required.

Is this only for animation?

No. Pip is our featured example. Send content from any YouTube niche so we can review what it needs.

How is my quote calculated?

The free sample is 90 seconds in 1 language. The Pilot is €49 for one full episode, up to 20 minutes, in 2 languages. Starter is €390/month for up to 120 video minutes and 3 languages. Channel is €690/month for up to 240 video minutes and 4 languages. Growth is €1,090/month for up to 360 video minutes and 6 languages, with a 48-hour turnaround and 2 extra translated title options per video. The back catalog is €690 one-off for 20 existing videos in 2 languages. An extra language is +€150/month on a monthly plan; for the back catalog, ask us. Larger networks and multiple channels are a custom quote. Prices exclude VAT where applicable. We confirm your total before starting.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled any month. The Pilot and the back catalog are one-off payments.

Which languages should I choose?

Starter includes 3, Channel 4 and Growth 6. The Pilot and the back catalog include 2. An extra language is +€150/month on a monthly plan. For an extra language on the back catalog, ask us. You can change languages from month to month.

Do I get the .srt subtitle files?

Yes. Every video gets timed .srt closed captions in your original language and in each new language. We upload them to YouTube for you, and you also receive the .srt files to keep and reuse on other platforms.

Do you guarantee more views?

No, and nobody honestly can. Views depend on your content, your audience and YouTube. We promise the work: AI dubbing, subtitles, translated titles, descriptions and chapters, and uploading in your YouTube Studio.

Are the voices human or AI?

The dubbed voices are AI, with a quality check. This is not human dubbing. ToonGhuri Studio combines an AI production pipeline with a team that checks every job. How the work is done

Why not just use YouTube’s automatic dubbing?

Where it’s available, YouTube’s automatic dubbing covers the audio. We handle the whole release: AI voices with your original music and effects, synced subtitles, translated titles, descriptions and chapters, and uploading in your YouTube Studio.

Do I share my YouTube password?

No. For uploads, you invite our team through YouTube Studio’s channel permissions.
