Good to know

Quick answers.

Anything else? Email studio@toonghuri.com.

What does the free sample include?

A free 90-second dub of your own video in one language you choose, so you can hear the result before paying anything. One free sample per new customer; no plan required.

Can every video use A/B testing?

We check your channel and video eligibility first. YouTube’s native tests require advanced features and do not support Shorts, private, age-restricted or Made for Kids videos. We set up eligible tests and review the outcome, including when there is no clear winner. See YouTube’s requirements (opens in a new tab) ↗

Is this only for animation?

No. Pip is our featured example. Send content from any YouTube niche so we can review what it needs.

How is my quote calculated?

Your first video is $39 for up to 20 minutes in 5 languages. The Channel plan is $590/month for 8 uploads (240 video minutes, up to 10 languages) and the Growth plan is $990/month for 12 uploads (360 video minutes, up to 12 languages), with the full package: dubbing, subtitles, translated titles and descriptions, chapter timestamps, 3 thumbnail variants, title variants, publishing, A/B test setup and results review where eligible, and a monthly results report. Up to 15 languages, videos over 60 minutes and larger libraries receive a custom Studio quote. We confirm your total before starting.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled any month. Anything beyond your plan’s uploads, minutes or languages is quoted before production starts.

Which languages should I choose?

You choose up to 10 (Channel) or 12 (Growth). Most channels get the biggest reach from a handful of large audiences, such as Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi and Indonesian, so we recommend starting with the 4–6 that fit your channel and audience, then adding more. You can change languages from month to month.

Do I get the .srt subtitle files?

Yes. Every video gets timed .srt closed captions in your original language and in each new language. We upload them to YouTube for you, and you also receive the .srt files to keep and reuse on other platforms.

Do you guarantee more views?

No, and nobody honestly can. Views depend on your content, your audience and YouTube. We guarantee the work: dubbed audio, subtitles, translated titles, descriptions and chapters, thumbnail and title variants, publishing, and test setup where eligible. Then we show you what happened, including tests with no clear winner.

Are the voices human or AI?

The dubbed voices are AI-generated, not human voice actors. ToonGhuri Studio combines an AI production pipeline with a team that oversees every job. How the work is done

Why not just use YouTube’s automatic dubbing?

Where it’s available, YouTube’s automatic dubbing covers the audio. We handle the whole release: distinct voices with your original music and effects, synced subtitles, translated titles, descriptions and chapters, thumbnail and title variants, and publishing and testing in your YouTube Studio.

Do I share my YouTube password?

No. For uploads, you invite our team through YouTube Studio’s channel permissions.
