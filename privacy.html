Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data ToonGhuri Studio collects when you visit this website, request a free sample, or use our YouTube localization service, why we use it, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who we are

ToonGhuri Studio is a YouTube localization agency based at Sofias Sliman 30, 11526 Ambelokipi, Athens, Greece. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for all privacy questions: studio@toonghuri.com.

2. Data you send us through the sample form

When you submit the “free sample” form on our homepage, we receive:

  • the YouTube video or channel link you enter;
  • your email address;
  • your name or channel name (optional);
  • the languages you would like to hear;
  • the offer or plan you selected (for example “Free sample” or “Full release package”);
  • the date and time of the submission and the IP address it was sent from.

Our form handler stores each request in a protected log file on our web server, which is not publicly accessible, and sends an email notification with the same details (except the IP address) to studio@toonghuri.com. If the form cannot reach our server, your browser instead prepares an email that you can choose to send us yourself; in that case we receive whatever that email contains.

We use this information to prepare your sample, reply to you, and discuss a quote. We do not use it for newsletters or advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Data we process when we work on your videos

If you order a sample or a paid service, we process the material needed to produce it, which can include:

  • your video, its audio, and any voices, names or images of people that appear in it;
  • transcripts, translations, subtitles, titles, descriptions, chapter timestamps and thumbnails we create from it;
  • channel and video information visible to us through the YouTube Studio permission you grant (for example video settings and analytics needed to review A/B tests);
  • the name and email address of you or your team members, and our correspondence;
  • billing details needed for invoices and payments.

You never share your YouTube password with us. You invite our team through YouTube Studio’s channel permissions, and you can remove that access at any time.

4. Legal bases

  • Steps before a contract and performing a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): answering your sample request, preparing quotes, and producing and publishing what you ordered.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): keeping our form and website secure (including logging IP addresses to detect abuse), keeping a record of enquiries, and handling questions or disputes. You can object to this processing at any time (see section 9).
  • Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): keeping invoices and accounting records as required by law.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): only where we ask for it specifically, for example before showing your video as an example on our website. You can withdraw consent at any time.

If the people who appear or speak in your videos are identifiable, you are responsible for having the right to share that material with us for localization (see our Terms of Service).

5. Service providers and third parties

We use the following providers. Some of them may process data outside the European Economic Area; where they do, we rely on the safeguards they offer under the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

  • Web hosting and form email: Hostinger hosts this website, stores the form log and sends the form notification email.
  • Email: Our email service provider handles our inbox.
  • Google Fonts (Google): this website loads its fonts from Google’s servers. When a page opens, your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address and basic browser information. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube (Google): the example video on our homepage is shown as a still image until you press play. Only then does your browser load the YouTube player, and YouTube may set cookies and collect usage data under its own policies. Links to YouTube open YouTube’s website.
  • Google AI services: we use Google’s AI services (the Gemini API, paid tier) to transcribe, translate and analyse your video and to generate the AI voices for your dub. Your video, audio and text are sent to Google for this purpose.
  • YouTube Studio (Google): we publish the agreed audio tracks, subtitles, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and tests to your channel through YouTube Studio, under YouTube’s terms and privacy policy.
  • Payments and invoicing: Stripe processes payments and sends invoices; it receives your billing details under its own privacy policy.

We may also disclose data where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies of its own. The Google Fonts request described above happens on every page. YouTube cookies are only set if you press play on the example video.

7. How long we keep data

  • Sample requests that do not lead to an order: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client videos, audio and working files: 90 days after delivery, unless you ask us to keep them for future work or delete them sooner.
  • Invoices and accounting records: as long as Greek tax and accounting law requires (generally at least 5 years).
  • YouTube Studio access: until you remove our team from your channel permissions.

8. Security

We limit access to your data to what is needed for your project, protect the form log from public access, and use channel permissions instead of passwords. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict how we use your data;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing before the withdrawal.

To use these rights, email studio@toonghuri.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work, or with our lead authority: the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).

10. Automated decisions

We use AI tools to produce translations, voices and other deliverables, but we do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our service or providers change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
